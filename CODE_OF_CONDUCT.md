# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in LOOPTAG to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence when a claim affects funds or safety.
- Give and accept constructive review.
- Respect scoped issues and assigned work. Do not duplicate or snipe another
  contributor's active PR.
- Credit prior work and link to sources.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting low-quality or bad-faith work, including fake test
  evidence or split PRs intended to farm rewards.
- Publicly disclosing an exploitable vulnerability before a fix is available.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer. Maintainers may remove comments,
close contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participation. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
