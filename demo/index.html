The physical tag

Serial-bound, not bearer-bound

The QR carries the contract, claim id, and a SHA-256 hash of the serial. The station must still read the item's serial and submit the hash. A copied tag from another item fails the on-chain check.

Verify it yourself

Do not trust this page

The page reads a committed manifest. The chain is the source of truth. Open the contract on Stellar Expert, then run the read-only commands below. 

stellar contract invoke \
  --id <contract-id> --network testnet -- \
  get_claim --claim_id 1

stellar contract invoke \
  --id <contract-id> --network testnet -- \
  get_merchant --merchant <issuer>
